# Changelog

One line for each change a visitor, Umair or a future agent would notice, newest first. Commits have the detail; this is the readable history. Put the date and, where useful, the commit or PR.

## 2026-11-27

- The glows on the controls are gone again, for good, and so is the light that ran along the speed slider and the import bar (D35). The only glow left is the light behind the page that dances to the music, on `themes` under `glow effect`, which starts at `off`. The sliders keep their colours.
- A first visit is now a black page in `rose`'s colours with the `glow effect` at `off`, at Umair's request (D34); it was paper in `rose` with all of the glow. Anyone who chose a look before keeps it. The glows that go with the music are back as they were, the ring turning behind play, the glow under the speed slider, on the grips, past the reverb slider and round the waveform, but only as far as `glow effect` on `themes` is turned up, so with it off nothing on the page glows. The link preview picture is the wordmark on black.
- Everything that made sloverb look generated is gone, at Umair's request (D33). The box to drop a song on and the YouTube link box have a plain line round them instead of a glowing edge in three colours. The wordmark is one still colour, the colour of the page's words. Play has no blurred ring behind it, the sliders, their grips, the waveform and save no longer glow, and save is filled with the colour of the page's words like `browse files` and play. The toast and the sheet shown while a file is held over the page are solid instead of frosted, a change of screen is a plain fade, and nothing grows under a mouse. The link preview picture is the plain wordmark on paper, now `og-2.jpg`. The light behind the page while a song plays is as it was.
- The site now loads Vercel Web Analytics, so page views, countries, browsers and devices show in the Analytics tab of the `sloverb` project on Vercel once it is enabled there. No cookies and nothing about songs (D32).
- A save can be stopped: while it is being made, `cancel` stands where `reset` is, just above save, and nothing is downloaded (D31).
- On the start screen the rule between `info` and `themes` is on the middle of the screen, in line with `or` and the `cancel` under a YouTube import above it, where it had been 15px to the left (D31).
- The YouTube import bar fills steadily from start to finish instead of leaping almost to the end as soon as the PC picks the job up and waiting there. Its words are now `connecting`, `finding`, `downloading`, `converting` and `loading` (D30).
- The worker on the PC runs this build since the installer was run there on 2026-11-27, so the journal and the retry below are live. The key, the pause setting and the boot task are as they were. The first import through it worked from link to MP3, and the relay's events arrived with their country.
- Fixed: on Windows the journal could not be read from an ordinary terminal, so `watch`, `stats` and `dashboard` showed nothing while the worker was writing it. Python turns the folder's mode `0o700` into permissions for its owner, and what the boot task creates is owned by the Administrators group, not the person. The folder now has the permissions of the one it is in, and the installer's approved step repairs one made the old way. Found on the PC minutes after the first install and fixed before anything was lost.
- Fixed: a YouTube link that came back `youtube blocked this import` and then worked when pasted again. The worker now tries a refused download again itself, up to three goes, before anyone is told, and writes down what yt-dlp said each time so the cause can be found (D29). An age-restricted video now says `that video isn't available`.
- The worker now keeps a journal of every YouTube import: who asked (address, country, city if the database is installed, browser), which video, its title and channel, how long each step took, how it ended and, when it failed, what yt-dlp really said. On the PC, `sloverb-worker.cmd watch` shows requests as they arrive, `dashboard` opens the same in a browser with charts of days, hours, songs, places and outcomes, and `stats`, `songs` and `imports` print the sums. `worker.log` now says in plain lines what the worker is doing. The relay only passes the notes on and keeps nothing, and nothing changed for visitors (D28, `docs/analytics.md`).
